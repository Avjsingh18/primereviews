Let’s find your Google review link and add it to PrimeReviews. Use the link that opens the review form for your business.

On a computer, go to business dot google dot com. Sign in with the Google account that manages your business, then select the correct business profile.

Check the business name and location. In your Business Profile, select Read reviews.

Next, select Get more reviews. This opens the options for sharing your review link.

Choose Copy beside the review link. Copy the full link Google provides. The address shown here is only an example.

Return to PrimeReviews. Paste the copied link into the Google review URL field, then select Continue.

Open your copied link in another browser tab. Check that it leads to the review form for your business. Customers may need to sign in to Google to post.

If the options are missing, check that you are signed in as an owner or manager of the correct profile. Google’s screen labels can vary. You are ready to continue with PrimeReviews.
